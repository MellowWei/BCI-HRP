00 / Overview
00
What this site is

HRP, retold from the user's side.

The Human Return Protocol (HRP) is an early-stage, falsifiable, adaptive auditory-AI research prototype for attention and emotional self-regulation. Other pages document its hardware concept, theoretical body, and laboratory validation. This site documents something else: the user-centered design research that frames why HRP exists and who it is for.

Human Return Protocol（HRP）是一个早期、可证伪的自适应声音-AI 研究原型，面向注意与情绪自我调节。其他站点记录了它的硬件概念、理论结构、与实验室验证路径。本站记录的是另一件事：让 HRP 之所以存在的以用户为中心的设计研究，以及它为谁而设计。

It is organized as a Design Thinking case study — Empathize, Define, How Might We, Ideate, Prototype, Test — with explicit pain points, journey maps, and validation metrics drawn from HCI and UX research practice.

本站按 Design Thinking 的研究流程组织：Empathize、Define、HMW、Ideate、Prototype、Test，包含明确的用户痛点、用户旅程地图，以及取自 HCI / UX 研究实践的评估指标。

A boundary statement: HRP does not currently claim clinical efficacy, treatment, completed BCI functionality, or proven neural regulation. Statements about user experience are framed as design hypotheses to be tested, not findings.

边界声明：HRP 目前不声称任何临床疗效、医学治疗、已完成 BCI 功能或已证明的神经调节。关于用户经验的陈述是待测试的设计假设，而非已经验证的结论。

01 / Empathize
01
Who and what

Before productivity fails, access to self fails.

The target user is not someone who is "underperforming." It is someone whose body, attention, and emotion have moved out of mutual reach — and who can no longer locate the thread that leads back to themselves.

目标用户并不是"表现不佳"的人。它是一个身体、注意、情绪相互失联的人——一个无法再找到回到自己那条线索的人。

"I know I should focus, but I don't know what I'm feeling."

"我知道我应该集中注意力。但我不知道我现在感觉是什么。"

Lived experience of the fragmented state

Attention does not simply scatter. It scatters across a body that has stopped sending readable signals, a clock that has lost its rhythm, a screen that demands action without comprehension, and an inner narration that has begun to translate every signal into shame. The user does not need optimization. The user needs a thread back.

注意不只是分散。它分散在一个不再发出可读信号的身体里、一个失去节律的时间里、一个要求行动却没有理解的屏幕前、一段把每个信号翻译成羞耻的内在独白里。这个用户需要的不是优化。这个用户需要的是一根回去的线索。

What current tools are not designed for

Productivity software assumes the user can name what they want to do. Wellness apps assume the user can name what they feel. Clinical pathways assume the user is willing to be diagnosed. AI chat assumes the user has language available. HRP is designed for the moments before any of those assumptions hold.

效率软件假设用户能说出自己想做什么。健康应用假设用户能说出自己的感受。临床路径假设用户愿意被诊断。AI 对话假设用户有语言可用。HRP 的设计针对的是这些假设都还不成立之前的那一段。

02 / Pain Points
02
Eight gaps

Eight design gaps current tools leave open.

Each card maps a real pain point to what existing productivity / wellness / AI tools miss, and to a corresponding HRP design opportunity. These are framed as design hypotheses to be tested, not as findings.

每张卡片把一个真实痛点对应到现有效率 / 健康 / AI 工具的盲点，并对应一个 HRP 的设计机会。这些是有待测试的设计假设，而不是已经验证的结论。

01

Fragmented Attention

注意力碎裂

"I open three apps and forget why I opened any of them."

"我打开了三个 App，然后想不起来我为什么打开它们。"

What current tools miss

Focus apps measure time-on-task; they cannot detect when attention has detached from intention. Productivity dashboards score output; they cannot show why coherence broke.

HRP design opportunity

Treat attention as a rhythmic field with multiple states (in-flow / drifting / overloaded / numb / stuck), not as a single performance metric. Sample the state, then offer a small return cue, not a deadline.

02

Emotional Overload

情绪过载

"There is too much feeling. I cannot pick which one to address first."

"感受太多了，我不知道该先处理哪一个。"

What current tools miss

Mood trackers ask the user to label emotion at the exact moment when language is least available. Meditation apps offer global calming protocols rather than situated ones.

HRP design opportunity

Allow non-verbal state sampling (rhythm, body, sound preference). Offer a low-density rhythm intervention before asking for a label. Language can come after, if at all.

03

Transition Failure

状态转换失败

"I cannot get from where I am to where I need to be next."

"我没办法从现在的状态，转到下一个我应该进入的状态。"

What current tools miss

Calendars and to-do lists assume the user can context-switch on command. They do not provide a transition layer between states.

HRP design opportunity

Design transitions as auditory-AI sequences (e.g., one rhythm to soften pressure, one to re-orient, one to re-enter), tested as discrete UX flows rather than as one global mode.

04

Body-Signal Blindness

身体信号不可读

"I notice I'm tired only after I have already crashed."

"我意识到自己累了，往往是已经撑不住之后了。"

What current tools miss

Most wearables surface fitness scores, not interpretable somatic cues. Numerical readiness scores can become a new kind of pressure rather than a return cue.

HRP design opportunity

Translate physiological signals (HRV, EDA, temperature, micro-motion — initially planned through the 44271 Neural Cuff layer) into rhythm-state inferences with explicit uncertainty, not into productivity scores.

05

Shame-Based Productivity Tools

羞耻驱动的效率工具

"Every notification feels like an evaluation."

"每一条通知都像一次评分。"

What current tools miss

Streaks, scores, and dashboards convert any deviation into a sign of failure. Users in fragmented states experience this as compounded shame, not as motivation.

HRP design opportunity

Replace evaluative feedback with reflective feedback. The system describes a state and offers a cue; it does not score the user. No streaks. No leaderboards. No public visibility by default.

06

AI Without Somatic Context

没有身体语境的 AI

"The AI gives me a perfect answer to a question my body cannot ask."

"AI 给了我一个完美答案——但回答的是一个我身体根本提不出来的问题。"

What current tools miss

General-purpose AI chat is fluent in language but blind to the bodily state of the person typing. It optimizes responses for legibility, not for somatic appropriateness.

HRP design opportunity

Pair AI feedback (AiAiQ<10 — AiQ愛<3 stabilization mode and AiQ愛<7 expansion mode) with state sampling, so the AI's register adjusts to the user's bandwidth, not the other way around.

07

Diagnostic Pressure

诊断压力

"I do not want a label. I want to be able to come back."

"我不想要一个标签。我只想能够回来。"

What current tools miss

Many wellness pathways funnel users toward clinical labels before the user is ready, willing, or in a context where a label is helpful. Labels can become identities that close return paths.

HRP design opportunity

Use functional rhythm-state descriptions instead of medical labels. State labels are framed as candidate states; the user remains the final interpretive authority. Crisis referrals exist as a separate, opt-in layer.

08

Surveillance Fatigue

被监控的疲惫

"I am tired of being measured."

"我累了，不想再被测量了。"

What current tools miss

Continuous tracking can shift the user's relationship to their own body from inhabitation to inspection. The very act of measuring becomes another form of pressure.

HRP design opportunity

Privilege on-device processing, minimum data retention, user-deletable history, and explicit "off" states. The protocol is offered, not imposed; the user can leave it without losing access to their own self-description.

03 / Define
03
Problem statement

Current tools assume coherent users. The user HRP is designed for is the user before coherence.

Productivity, wellness, and AI tools are largely designed around a coherent user — a user who can name their goal, name their feeling, and select an intervention. Users in fragmented attentional and emotional states often cannot. They are filtered out of the entire interaction surface. HRP is designed to meet this user before coherence, not after — and to support a return without diagnosing them, shaming them, or forcing them into productivity logic.

效率类、健康类、AI 类工具的默认用户是已经连贯的用户——能说出目标、说出感受、选出干预的用户。处于注意/情绪碎片状态的用户往往做不到这些，于是被这些工具的整个交互面过滤掉了。HRP 的设计恰恰针对这个被过滤掉的用户：在连贯之前与他相遇，而不是之后；在不诊断他、不让他羞耻、不强迫他进入效率逻辑的前提下，支持一次返回。

Users experiencing attention or emotional fragmentation need a way to recognize and regulate their state without being diagnosed, shamed, or forced into productivity logic.

处于注意 / 情绪碎片状态的用户，需要一种方式去识别和调节自己的状态——而不必经过诊断、不必产生羞耻、也不必被压回效率逻辑。

04 / How Might We
04
Six framing questions

Six How Might We questions HRP attempts to answer.

Each HMW question converts a pain point into a research-able design problem. They are framed broadly enough to admit multiple solutions, narrowly enough to be testable.

每一个 HMW 把一个痛点转化成一个可以研究的设计问题。它们的范围足够大，能容纳多种解决方案；又足够具体，能被检验。

HMW · 01

How might we help users identify their state without applying diagnostic labels?

如何帮助用户识别自己的状态——而不为他们贴上诊断标签？

HMW · 02

How might we turn body signals into gentle feedback rather than surveillance?

如何让身体信号成为温和的反馈，而不是监控？

HMW · 03

How might we use rhythm and sound as a bridge between overwhelm and action?

如何让节律与声音成为从过载到行动之间的桥梁？

HMW · 04

How might we design AI feedback that supports agency rather than dependency?

如何设计支持主体性而非制造依赖的 AI 反馈？

HMW · 05

How might we help users transition between states instead of demanding immediate productivity?

如何帮助用户在状态之间转换，而不是要求立刻进入效率？

HMW · 06

How might we validate HRP scientifically without flattening lived experience into empty metrics?

如何对 HRP 进行科学验证，而不把亲历经验压扁成空洞的指标？

05 / User Journey
05
Six stages of return

The return journey, in six stages.

The HRP user journey is mapped from the moment something fragments to the moment a partial return occurs. Each stage carries its own pain points, design opportunities, and validation metrics. The journey is non-linear — users can re-enter at any stage — and partial returns are treated as design success, not as failure.

HRP 的用户旅程从某个东西开始碎裂的一刻开始，到一次"部分返回"为止。每个阶段都有它自己的痛点、设计机会与评估指标。旅程是非线性的——用户可以在任何阶段重新进入——部分返回被视为设计成功，而不是失败。

Stage 01 · Trigger

Something fragments

触发：某个东西碎裂了

"Something just shifted, and I'm not okay anymore."

User state

Sudden shift in attention, emotion, or bodily comfort. May not yet be nameable.

Pain point

No external system marks the shift. The user is still expected to keep performing.

HRP feature

Lightweight state sampling: the protocol can be opened and ask a non-verbal question (rhythm choice, sound preference, color of the field).

Validation metric

Time from trigger to first state sample; willingness to open the protocol when distressed.

Stage 02 · Confusion

Language is not yet available

混乱：语言还没准备好

"I don't know what I want. I don't even know what I am."

User state

Aware that something is off, unable to label it.

Pain point

Mood trackers and AI chat both demand the user provide language they do not yet have.

HRP feature

Non-verbal sampling pathways. Candidate state labels offered as suggestions, not assignments. Option to choose "I don't know yet."

Validation metric

Confusion-rating drop after sampling; user willingness to continue without naming.

Stage 03 · Search

Looking for a thread back

寻找：找一根回去的线索

"I want something — a sound, a sentence, anything — to help me remember who I was a minute ago."

User state

Searching for a return cue; tolerance for friction is low.

Pain point

Wellness apps load slowly, ask for setup, or surface evaluative metrics first.

HRP feature

Direct rhythm recommendation based on sampled state; AiQ愛<3 stabilization mode for low-bandwidth language; minimal-friction entry.

Validation metric

Time-to-first-cue; perceived appropriateness of the cue (Likert).

Stage 04 · Intervention

The cue lands

介入：线索落地

"This sound matches what I am, even though I couldn't have asked for it."

User state

Engaged with rhythm / AI feedback. Body begins to register.

Pain point

Generic interventions feel impersonal; over-prescriptive ones feel coercive.

HRP feature

Adaptive auditory intervention; AiQ愛<7 expansion mode for users who want deeper reasoning; user can adjust intensity, exit, or switch.

Validation metric

Perceived appropriateness; pre-post change on subjective focus / emotion / body tension.

Stage 05 · Return

A partial coming-back

返回：部分地回来了

"I am not better. I am back enough to do one thing."

User state

Functional return. Not euphoric, not "fixed" — back enough.

Pain point

Productivity tools demand full readiness; users who are partially back drop out.

HRP feature

"Minimum real action" output: one suggested next step, sized to current bandwidth, not to optimal performance.

Validation metric

Self-reported "return-to-self" score; willingness to take one next step.

Stage 06 · Reflection

Memory of return

反思：返回的记忆

"Last time this happened, this is what helped."

User state

Optional retrospective sense-making. May happen hours or days later.

Pain point

Most apps lose the link between what happened and what worked.

HRP feature

Personal Rhythm Map — a private accumulation of "what helped me return." Editable. Deletable. Not a score.

Validation metric

User-reported sense of self-knowledge over time; ability to articulate one's own return cues to others.

07 / Prototype
07
Prototype v0

Prototype v0: enough for UX testing, not yet enough for neural claims.

The current prototype is a software-based research instrument. It supports user-experience testing, pain-point interviews, and pilot subjective-measure collection. It does not yet support physiological or neural claims; those require the Phase 3 lab collaboration.

目前的原型是一个软件型的研究工具。它能支持用户体验测试、痛点访谈和主观测量的初步采集。它还不能支持任何生理或神经层面的主张——那需要第三阶段的实验室合作。

What is currently built

AiAiQ<10 operating portal — dual-AI portal (AiQ愛<3 / AiQ愛<7); reflective AI feedback; mode switching; subjective state capture; rhythm recommendation; pilot logging.

Genre-driven intervention matrix — six music profiles (Breakbeats, Hyperpop, Synthpop, Drift Phonk, Darkwave, Ambient Techno) plus the 427Hz anchor, defined as operational sound profiles rather than aesthetic decoration.

Subjective state instruments — pre/post questions on focus, emotion, body tension, inner noise, task readiness, and "return-to-self."

Behavioral proxy signals — scroll velocity, click density, dwell time, and input rhythm as low-cost behavioral state-sampling pending hardware.

What is currently simulated

44271 Neural Cuff — described as a planned wearable physiological sensing layer. The website hosts hardware specifications and design semantics, but the device is not yet a manufactured product. Its sensing layer is currently simulated through behavioral proxies.

QDR Engine inference — the rule-based version operates on subjective inputs; the multi-modal physiological version awaits the lab partnership.

What is planned

Phase 3 physiological / neural validation with a partner laboratory: HRV / EDA / EEG / fNIRS layered onto the existing four-condition pilot design (baseline / fixed rhythm / random rhythm / adaptive rhythm + AiAiQ<10).

IRB-approved data collection through UPenn IRB Social/Behavioral Review (faculty sponsor: Susan Yoon, Penn GSE) before any human-subject data is collected.

Why this is enough for early UX testing

UX research at this stage tests whether the design framing maps onto real user experience — not whether the system regulates the brain. Phase 0 usability tests, Phase 1 pain-point interviews, and Phase 2 subjective-measure micro-pilots are appropriately matched to the prototype's current capabilities. Neural claims wait for Phase 3.

现阶段的 UX 研究测试的是设计框架是否对应真实用户经验——不是系统是否在调节大脑。第 0 阶段的可用性测试、第 1 阶段的痛点验证访谈、第 2 阶段的主观指标 micro-pilot 与当前原型能力匹配；任何关于神经层面的主张都要等第 3 阶段。

08 / Test
08
Four-phase plan

A four-phase UX research plan, separate from the scientific pilot.

The plan separates UX-research validation (Phases 0–2) from physiological / neural validation (Phase 3). This separation lets each phase carry its own falsification conditions; design assumptions can be revised without conflating them with physiological claims.

本计划把 UX 研究的验证（0–2 阶段）与生理 / 神经层面的验证（第 3 阶段）分开，让每个阶段有各自的可证伪条件——设计假设可以被修正，而不必与生理主张混在一起。

Phase 00
Usability Test
N = 3–5

Can a first-time user navigate the protocol without instruction?

第一次接触的用户能否在没有引导的情况下使用该原型？

Goal

Locate friction points in the interaction flow before larger sample testing.

Procedure

Think-aloud protocol; 3–5 participants attempt one full session; observer logs hesitations, recoveries, and exit points.

Metrics

Task completion rate; time-to-first-cue; confusion ratings (1–7).

Falsifier

If >50% of participants cannot complete one session unaided, the interaction model fails its first design test and needs revision before Phase 1.

Phase 01
Pain-Point Interviews
N = 5–8

Do the assumed pain points map onto real user experience?

假设中的痛点是否对应到真实用户经验？

Goal

Validate, refine, or replace the eight pain-point cards using semi-structured interviews.

Procedure

45-minute interviews; recruit users in fragmented attentional / emotional states; thematic analysis with two coders; pre-registered coding scheme.

Metrics

Frequency of named pain points; novel pain points discovered; rank-ordering of severity.

Falsifier

If a pain point is not named by any participant in this sample, it should be downgraded or removed from the design framing — not "defended" against absence of data.

Phase 02
Subjective-Measure Micro-Pilot
N = 8–12

Does the prototype support any pre-post movement on subjective measures?

原型在主观测量上能否产生任何 pre-post 变化？

Goal

Generate honest, underpowered initial data on whether the adaptive condition produces any session-related change.

Procedure

Within-subject four-condition design (baseline / fixed rhythm / random rhythm / adaptive + AiAiQ<10); pre/post self-report; behavioral task; user notes.

Metrics

Pre-post deltas on focus / emotion / body tension / return-to-self; effect-size estimates (with explicit acknowledgement of low power).

Falsifier

If adaptive does not differ in direction from baseline / fixed / random across this small sample, the loop assumption needs revision before scaling to Phase 3.

Phase 03
Lab Collaboration
N = TBD

Do physiological / neural measures converge with subjective and behavioral findings?

生理 / 神经测量与主观、行为测量是否收敛？

Goal

Test the loop assumption at the physiological / neural layer with a partner laboratory.

Procedure

HRV / EDA / EEG / fNIRS layered onto the four-condition design; pre-registered analysis plan; data sharing under a partner-side IRB and data-handling agreement.

Metrics

Pre-registered effect sizes on HRV variance, EDA modulation, EEG alpha/theta/beta, fNIRS prefrontal oxygenation; convergence with subjective measures.

Falsifier

If physiological / neural measures fail to align in direction with subjective and behavioral measures across replication, the system's inferred loop is not supported and the framing must be revised — not the data.

09 / Design Validation Metrics
09
UX / HCI evaluation

Ten metrics for evaluating the design, not the brain.

These metrics evaluate HRP as an HCI / UX artefact. They are distinct from the physiological / neural metrics in Phase 3. Both layers must hold for HRP to be considered well-designed and well-validated.

这些指标用来评估 HRP 作为 HCI / UX 作品本身，与第 3 阶段的生理 / 神经指标不同。HRP 想要被认定为设计良好且经过验证，两个层次都要成立。

01

Completion rate

完成率

Share of participants who complete one session without external help.

02

Time to understand project

理解项目所需时间

Median time from landing on the site to a 1-sentence accurate paraphrase of HRP.

03

Confusion rating

困惑评分

Self-report (1–7) on how confused the participant felt at any stage. Reversed for analysis.

04

Perceived agency

主体性感受

Self-report on whether the system felt like it served the user, or like the user served the system.

05

Perceived support

支持感

Felt-presence rating; whether the AI / rhythm felt like accompaniment rather than instruction.

06

Perceived scientific credibility

科研可信度感受

Whether participants from research backgrounds rate the framing as honest about its boundaries.

07

Perceived shame reduction

羞耻感降低

Pre-post change on shame ratings during a session involving fragmentation.

08

Willingness to use again

再次使用的意愿

Likelihood of opening the protocol the next time fragmentation occurs.

09

Ability to explain HRP

能否复述 HRP

Participants asked to explain HRP in one sentence; rated for accuracy and absence of overclaim.

10

Trust without dependency

信任而不依赖

Composite measure: trust in the system + retained agency. High-trust + low-dependency is the design target.

11 / Tokyo Collaboration

Why this design-research layer matters to a neuroscience / BCI / NeuroAI lab.

HRP is not only a technical system. It is a user-centered neuro-AI design research project. The goal is to validate both lived user need and physiological / neural mechanism — neither layer alone is sufficient. We invite collaboration with neuroscience, BCI, and NeuroAI laboratories for experimental rigor at the sensing layer and for scientific validation at the physiological / neural layer.

Specifically: the four-condition pilot (baseline / fixed rhythm / random rhythm / adaptive rhythm + AiAiQ<10) is designed to be layerable with HRV, EDA, EEG, and fNIRS. UPenn-side IRB documentation will be shared with partner-side IRB committees as soon as it is filed.

Why this matters

What this design-research layer changes.

Is adding UX research necessary?

Yes. Without it, HRP reads as a system in search of users. With it, HRP reads as a research project that knows who it is for and how it will be evaluated.

是的。没有这一层，HRP 看起来像一个寻找用户的系统。有了这一层，HRP 才看起来像一个清楚知道自己为谁、以及如何被评估的研究项目。

What happens if the site lacks user pain points?

The system appears speculative. Reviewers default to asking "for whom?" and the absence of an answer becomes the absence of legitimacy.

系统看起来是空想的。审稿人的默认问题是"为谁而做？"——而这个问题没有答案，本身就成了合法性的缺失。

What becomes stronger after adding design thinking?

Three things: the project becomes more fundable (a clear user + a clear test plan), more lab-readable (HCI/UX language is shared across labs), and more honest (design hypotheses can be falsified without dragging the underlying philosophy with them).

三件事：更可被资助（清晰的用户 + 清晰的测试计划），更可被实验室读懂（HCI/UX 语言是跨实验室共享的），更诚实（设计假设可以被证伪，而不必把背后的哲学一起拉下来）。

What is the single most important UX section to add first?

Pain points. Without them, every other section becomes decoration on a system whose purpose has not been argued. Pain points are the only section that can be wrong, and the willingness to be wrong is the precondition for being trusted.

用户痛点。没有它，其他每一段都只是一个还没被论证过目的的系统的装饰。痛点是唯一可能出错的那一段——而愿意可能出错，是被信任的前提。
